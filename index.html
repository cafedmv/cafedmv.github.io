What We Do

WHAT WE DO

1. We improve desolate spaces through community (public) art projects

We do public space design and renovation to make our communities look and feel brighter. This leads to creating a "walkable" city through art! From mural and street art to social movement events and programs, we're up to snuff on our gameplan.

2. We cultivate culture and community through art education

We host community workshops, children's community art programs, and even workshops for seasoned artists, teachers and community leaders. We've got something fun for everyone to enjoy!

3. We create space for cultural exchange within the community

We're all about showcasing the best in all of us through the community exchange of nationalities, race, gender and generations through art exhibitions, performances, festivals, and cultural events.
