Journey of Belonging

What you see is rarely the whole story. This bench is a space to bridge the gap between how we are perceived and who we truly are. It is a physical map of our assumptions — and a chance to move past them.

How to Participate

  1. Take a Seat. Sit on opposite sides of the glass with a partner or a stranger.
  2. Choose a Prompt. Pick a question below. One person reads it aloud.
  3. The Guuess & The Reveal.
    • The Observer. Point to where you think the other person falls on the scale.
    • The Participant. Point to your actual truth.
  4. See the Gap. Look at the distance between your fingers. This is the space between perception and reality.

Note: These scales are not just two options—they are a spectrum. Use the full length of the glass to find the exact spot that reflects your truth. If your fingers are far apart, take a moment to ask: "What made you see me that way?"
