Our Projects

Educational Programs

Detroit, DC Metro Area, Remote

From mental health to art workshops, we offer accessible programs that anyone can jump into and shine. We design our programs to foster creativity, connection, and a positive community environment so everyone can equally express themselves and learn from each other.

Murals

Detroit, DC Metro Area

With over 5 years of experience painting on the public canvas, we help organizations and neighborhoods express themselves through co-designed artwork that reflects their community. From churches to government buildings, we're ready to bring the paint!

Community Events

Detroit, DC Metro Area

We love bringing the community together. From hosting our own art days to adding art corners to block parties, we're flexible on how we bring joy to the neighborhood in all sorts of forms. Chalk sketching, cardboard house paintings, community sculptures; we've done it all!
